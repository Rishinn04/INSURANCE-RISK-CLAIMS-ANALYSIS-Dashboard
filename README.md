# INSURANCE-RISK-CLAIMS-ANALYSIS-Dashboard
An interactive insurance analytics dashboard for exploring claim amounts, policy trends, and risk insights across car usage, car makes, age groups, driving behavior, coverage zones, education, gender, marital status, and claim frequency. Built with data visualizations to support insurance risk analysis and claims monitoring.

## Project Overview

The **Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project designed to analyze insurance policies, claim amounts, and risk patterns across various customer and vehicle demographics. It provides key insights into claim distribution based on car usage, car make, coverage zone, age group, driving behavior, education level, gender, and marital status.
The dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs), including total policies, total claim amount, average claim frequency, and average claim amount, alongside visualizations that help identify trends and patterns in insurance claims over time.
The primary objective of this project is to transform raw insurance data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making, improve risk assessment, and help insurance companies better understand claim behavior and customer segments.


## Business Questions Answered
- What is the total number of insurance claims
- What is the total claim amount and average claim amount
- Which customer segments have the highest claim amounts
- How do claim amounts vary across different age groups
- Which factors are associated with higher insurance claim costs
- What trends and patterns can be identified in insurance claims
- Which categories contribute most to overall claim costs
- How can data insights support better insurance risk management

## Key Findings

- Analyzed 37,542 insurance policies with a total claim amount of 187.82M.
- Observed an average claim frequency of 0.51 and an average claim amount of 5,002.9.
- Private car use accounts for approximately 80.08% of the total claim amount, compared with 19.92% for commercial use.
- The 26–65 age groups contribute the highest claim amounts, at approximately 35M–36M per group.
- Ford and Chevrolet appear among the car makes with the highest total claim amounts in the displayed chart.
- Claim amounts vary across coverage zones, with each zone contributing a relatively similar share of the total.
- The yearly claim amount shows a noticeable increase around 1999 and remains relatively high through much of the following period.
- Bachelor's degree holders account for approximately 50% of the total claim amount.
- Single policyholders contribute the highest claim amount among the displayed marital-status categories.
- The dashboard enables interactive comparison of claim metrics by vehicle use, car make, age group, year, education, coverage zone, and marital status.
